# AR Sukkah — the week of the holiday

Sukkot 2026 begins at sundown **Friday 25 September** and runs to
**2 October**, with Shemini Atzeret and Simchat Torah to the 4th.

This file lives with the piece because the piece is annual. Next year the
dates change and everything below still works.

---

## Before anything goes out

**The Hebrew on the page has never been read by a Hebrew speaker.** It is in
`index.html`, in `COPY.he`. Check it first — a typo in the one language most
of the audience reads is worse than not shipping the Hebrew at all.

Specific doubts, so the check is quick:

| String | Now | The doubt |
|---|---|---|
| Button | `להציב אותה בחדר` | Infinitive. A button usually wants the imperative — `הציבו אותה בחדר` |
| Title | `סוכה במציאות רבודה` | Correct but long. People say "AR". Is `סוכה ב-AR` better on a phone? |
| No-AR notice | `…בטלפון — ספארי…` | Em dashes inside RTL can sit on the wrong side. Look at it on an actual phone |
| Lab link | `עוד במעבדה` | Fine, but it is your word for it, not mine |
| Credit | `פול רוזנבוים` | ✅ Verified — matches the events site exactly, 15 uses |

## When to post

Friday evening through Saturday night is dead, and the first day of the
holiday after it. Do not spend the launch there.

| Day | What |
|---|---|
| **Wed 23 or Thu 24** | The main post. Before everyone goes offline, so it is in people's hands to forward |
| Fri 25 | Nothing |
| **Sun 27 onward** | Chol HaMoed. Kids off school, everyone out visiting sukkot. This is when the thing actually gets used — second post, lighter |

## WhatsApp is the real channel

Not Instagram. In Israel this is a link forwarded into family groups while
people are sitting in someone's sukkah with a phone. Everything else exists
to start that.

Which means the **link preview is the most important asset**, not the
caption — and it is already good: the eye, the op-art walls, and the words
*All are welcome here / Across all dimensions*.

Send this to five people yourself on the Wednesday. That is the whole
distribution plan.

> בניתי סוכה שאפשר להציב בכל חדר, דרך הטלפון. בלי אפליקציה, נפתח בדפדפן.
> חג שמח 🌿
> ar-sukkah.unapaulogetic.art

> I built a sukkah you can stand in any room. Through the phone, no app —
> it just opens in the browser. Chag sameach 🌿
> ar-sukkah.unapaulogetic.art

on a floor, then a close-up of the eye.

## Twitch

You have been streaming a month, so use it rather than posting about it.

- Add a **panel** under the stream: title "AR Sukkah", the park photograph,
  the link. Two minutes, and it stays up all year
- On the next stream, open it on camera and put it on the floor of the room
  you are streaming from. That is a thirty-second bit and it is far better
  than any caption
- Put the link in the chat when you do, so it is forwardable from there

## The second post, chol hamoed

Lighter, and not from you. Ask people to send you where they put it — a
stairwell, a beach, an office. Repost what comes back.

> Where did you put yours?

That is the one that costs nothing and gets you the images for next year.
