AR Sukkah

On a phone: tap the button, then point the camera at the floor and move it slowly until the sukkah lands. One finger slides it across the floor. Two fingers turn it and change its size. On a computer: drag to turn it around.

To share it: the AR view has its own camera button, for photos and for video. Tag @unapaulogetic_ — I will repost it.

This browser will not open AR. Open the page on a phone — Safari on iPhone, or Chrome on Android — and the button appears.
